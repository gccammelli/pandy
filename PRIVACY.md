# Privacy

Pandy has no servers and collects nothing.

- **Your printer**: Pandy talks to it directly on your network. Its access code is stored on your phone only,
  encrypted with the Android Keystore.
- **Home Assistant** (if you add it): its address is stored on your phone, its token encrypted like the access code.
  Pandy only talks to the address you give.
- **Pandy Slicer** (if you set it up): runs on your own machine. Pandy sends it the model to slice and, so it can put the
  result on the printer, the printer's address, serial number and access code.
- **MakerWorld** (the Models tab): a web page from makerworld.com, with Bambu Lab's own privacy policy. Its sign-in stays
  in the page's cookies on your phone; Pandy only reads the files you choose to print.
- No analytics, no ads, no crash reporting.
